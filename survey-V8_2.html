Plan Subscription

Monthly subscription to General AI Agent on the app — drag each slider to set your price thresholds. The four points must remain in ascending order.

General AI Agent
GenericStandardizedEveryday Practice
  • Completed in ~30 seconds
  • Automatically analyzes your swing (phases, speed, angles, common issues) and provides suggestions
Coach AI Agent
Real-TimeAdaptive GuidanceCoach-Trained
  • Completed within seconds
  • Trained using real coaching examples and feedback
  • Learns the coach's decision-making, teaching priorities, and experience
  • Regularly reviewed and improved by the coach
Human Coach Analysis
Expert ReviewPersonalizedHigher Cost
  • Personally reviewed by a coach (up to ~10 minutes)
